⚔️ The Game: Elden Ring / Dark Souls × Chivalry

"I started DUAL because I wanted to unite the punishing atmosphere, dark fantasy dread, and exploration of Elden Ring / Dark Souls with the directional melee mastery, stamina dueling, and tactile weapon impact of Chivalry."

The Core Mechanic: Impermanence of the Flesh & Ghost Possession

In DUAL, the player's physical vessel is never permanent. When struck down in battle, you don't merely restart at a checkpoint. Instead, your soul detaches from the body, and you awaken as an ethereal wandering ghost/spirit cast across a foggy, perilous realm.

As a spirit, you journey through the mist to discover new dormant or defeated physical bodies across the map. Through the core mechanic PossessVessel, you inhabit these bodies, adapt to their unique weights and armaments, defeat dangerous adversaries, and seek to uncover the truth of a divided world where souls cannot cross to the other side.

Active Solo Development (2026~): Development began at the start of 2026 as a dedicated student research project. Both the gameplay systems and custom engine are continuously growing side by side.

⚙️ The Custom Engine: Why Build in C++ from Scratch?

Rather than relying on commercial black-box engines, I chose to build a custom game engine in C++ from the ground up. I wanted complete control over the rendering pipeline, shader systems, and memory layout—learning game development from the metal up.

A Hybrid ECS / OOP Foundation: Instead of dogmatic pure ECS, the engine uses a pragmatic Hybrid Component architecture. This allows me to write complex hierarchical combat state machines and AI intuitively, while maintaining component modularity and runtime flexibility for the dynamic PossessVessel mechanic.

The engine is built to grow directly alongside DUAL's game design. As features mature, deeper engine architecture deep-dives and technical breakdowns will be documented.

💡 Dev Reflections: What I Failed, What I Gained & What I Learned

Error / Struggle

What I Failed & Struggled With

  • Low-Level Complexity: Wrestling with matrix transforms, custom camera collisions, and shader debugging without visual tools led to countless game-crashing bugs early on.
  • Combat Spacing & Physics: Replicating Chivalry-style directional melee hitboxes from scratch in C++ was far harder than expected—early hitboxes clipped through terrain or lagged behind animations.
  • Scope Creep vs. Tooling: At first, I tried to write every engine feature under the sun before nailing the core possession loop. I had to rein myself in and focus on what the game actually needed.
Gained & Achieved

What I Gained

  • Deep C++ & Graphics Mastery: An intimate understanding of memory management, cache locality, shaders, and rendering pipelines that no high-level engine tutorial could ever teach.
  • Complete Architectural Freedom: Every single engine system is tailored precisely to DUAL's unique spirit possession mechanics—no fighting another engine's assumptions.
  • Creative Confidence: Seeing an armored knight walk across misty peaks rendered by my own engine code gave me unshakeable confidence as a solo student developer.
Takeaways

What I Learned

  • Build for the Game, Not for Dogma: A pragmatic hybrid architecture that lets you ship features easily beats pure architectural theory every day of the week.
  • Fail Fast & Iterate: The only way to truly understand a low-level engine bug is to break it, trace the pointers, and rebuild it stronger.
  • Turning Imagination into Reality: Big fantasy visions (inspired by donghua, anime, and manhwa) require deep technical discipline to become playable games.

⛩️ Game Concept: Fast Action × Card Strategy

"When given the jam theme 'Time and Again', we interpreted it as the Japanese proverb 「七転八起」 (Fall seven times, stand up eight) — translating 'again and again' into a time loop seamlessly woven into the worldbuilding and folklore."

Swift Cat Controls × Tactical Card Selection

廻猫 - MEINEKO - is a high-speed 2D action platformer where players control an agile spirit cat navigating a mist-shrouded shrine forest. Combining rapid acrobatics (wall climbs, quick dashes, vertical leaps) with real-time tactical card selection, the player activates sacred talisman cards (札) to unleash elemental slashes, barrier wards, and special traversal buffs.

The 9-Lives Time Loop: Reinterpreting the cat's nine lives through the lens of reincarnation and time loops, each defeat is not a failure, but a cycle of learning that advances the narrative and reveals the hidden truth behind the shrine: "Can you uncover the truth behind the story?"
